	- The importance of software architecture. We need to architecture to make cathedrals instead of pyramids. With the same materials, architecture can create a cathedral instead of pyramids. Your approach can't be simply scaling a dog house to a factor of thousand times.
	- He came up with the idea of pink and blue contexts. Pink context is the existing way of doing things. Blue context is a new way of doing things. It is important to have blue thoughts to come up with something radical. Thinking within a context is simply a mental optimization.
	- For the Alan Kay, the blue idea was from biology. From the cells which scale to all the modern life, we wanted to software to mimic cells. 
		- Came up with the idea of "You must, must not let the interior of any one of these things to be a factor in the computation of the whole"
			- Saw this pattern in Ivan Sutherland's Sketchpad, the Airforce and Simula. 
		- However what was more important was the message passing between them. He felt sorry for coining object oriented. He wanted to come up with something more process oriented name to focus on the relation between two objects. Also made reference to the Japanese word Ma. He wanted a universal interface language where objects can talk to each other at runtime achieving interoperability.

		- Live system which you can inspect was also his core motto. Similar to web's HMR. He found this to be essential for building complex systems. "Live Programming"
