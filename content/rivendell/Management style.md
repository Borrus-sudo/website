If I ever end up managing a team, these are the principles that I think I should follow:
- Small, effective team granting lots of agency to its members
- Having a common high level goal without getting fixated on the "how". As long as there is a shared goal, the way we get there does not matter.
- The corollary of the above point is to explore multiple paths of achieving the same thing to avoid going all-in on one approach.
- Focus on interdisciplinary people over interdisciplinary teams. Esp useful in the AI age.
- Problem rich environment is a must. If the work done is revolutionary, you can make a new field out of it!
