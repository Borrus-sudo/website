#principle 
- Software Pace Layers
    -  Intelligence (very fast moving)
    -  Apps
    -  Libraries
    -  Platforms
    -  Protocols
    -  Formats (very slow moving)
- Many companies have perished in trying to create a commercial "protocol" layer.
	- IBM failed with its bus technology (needs more expansion)
	- Gopher 
	- Adobe Flash too in some sense. Though Apple had a bigger role.
- Basis for [[File over app]] philosophy.
