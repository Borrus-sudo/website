==NOTE==: The following points are highly speculative in nature. However I think they serve as a wonderful mental model for Symbolic Thought. Also see [[Musings on Thought, Intelligence and Language]]. 

- The Language of Thought, popularly nick-named as mentalese is the "computational" or the "representational" theory of mind that posits that mind is like a Turing machine.
- Just how a Turing machine takes inputs from an infinite tape containing symbols and performs various operations by moving up and down the tape, the mind similarly is a processor that acts upon these symbols using a fixed set of rules to generate outputs. Ideas are represented as symbols in this system upon which the brain acts using a fix set of rules/reflexes to generate new pieces of symbols. This is essentially what we would call "reasoning". The **Language of Thought Hypothesis** posits just this! 
- Agreeing to this hypothesis would imply that thought is independent of language. Also it would imply that the mind has a tacit set of knowledge before-hand. One can also posit that our inherent biases and heuristics as described by Daniel Kahneman and Amor Tversky are baked into this system due to evolution. 
- There are of-course obvious downsides to this theory however I think it presents a neat little theory to explain "symbolic thinking" and connects to behavioral economics by explaining that evolution has afforded the part of brain concerned with mentalese to be built with the mis-leading heuristics and biases. 
- Metaphor is baked into this system and serves as a tool of inference. I disagree with Lakoff's view that metaphor is the foundation of thought. It is a tool that allows a human to remember two similarly looking symbolic structures together. Absolutely recommended to read Steven Pinker's chapter Metaphor Metaphor in the book Stuff of Thought!
- The mentalese could possess a few mental symbolic primitives and most of other symbols could be a [[Lego Model#^015cc3|composition]] of these primitives! 
- Abstraction evolved via metaphor. However killjoy theory is proof that we have such a symbolic system like mentalese in place!
