The invention of paper made it really easy to codify our languages into notations. We call this "writing". Writing further enabled a whole lot of human progress by serving as an extension to man's memory. The development of printing press ushered the industrial revolution and advent of computers made [hyperlinking](https://ribbonfarm.com/2009/07/01/the-rhetoric-of-the-hyperlink/) possible. **Languages**, **Paper**, **Printing Press**, **Computers** (hyperlinking) each of them can be thought of as a medium which had affordances that made it easy [^2] to have thoughts that weren't possible to have before. Hence as McLuhan says, it is important to study the properties of these mediums and not the messages inside them.
