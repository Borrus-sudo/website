- Tools vs Mediums. Tools are generally for a one-off task. Mediums are for a more general purpose one. 
		- Alan Kay thought of Computing as a medium which gives users the ability to shape their own tools
			- First we shape our tools and then they shape us. 
			- Humans are tool creators above all.
			- Creating software is like sculpting clay where the clay is the computer. 
		- Coding agents might finally help us achieve personal software tool goal. 
		- [[Future OS]] should be a medium where different tools can act in harmony along with an agent. These tools itself can be a medium. For e.g you have a tool that helps you explore category theory a bit and may-be a music composing software on the right where you are taking inspiration from category theory to come up with a novel music?
