- Boundary objects: 
	- Boundary objects are artifacts which can be used by different groups of people together without requiring them to look at it from the same perspective.
	- Each group has a local interpretation of the object that fits their needs and constraints.
	- Local interpretations are a subset of the global common identity of the boundary object.
	- E.g. ISA: hardware engineers tend to think of this as a spec to implement. Software engineers think of it as a spec to utilize. The spec is designed delicately to fit the needs of software whilst making sure that the underlying hardware can be delivered in the desired tradeoffs of Power, Performance and Area. Once designed, it becomes a boundary object with both set of engineers looking at with different mental models. Each of these mental models highlight an aspect of the ISA.
