 - CRC: Class, Responsibility and Collaboration. Invented by SmallTalk programmers. You take index cards of fixed dimensions and write upon it the class name, its responsibilities at a high level and the collaboration that it might have to do with other classes. If it didn't fit the index card, you are either not describing the class' responsiblities at a higher level or you need to refactor the class.
