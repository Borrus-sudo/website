- Analogy is the motor that drives categorization
- It is the information highway of cognition. 
- We have this unique ability to see similarities even in seemingly unfamiliar situations/ideas/concepts on the surface level. 
- We use analogy to form concepts and further higher level concepts. 
	- E.g. Shadow used in typical sense.
	- Shadow then used in rain-shadow sense, snow shadow. 
	- The word shadow got raised in its conceptual sense.
- Whenever we are trying to say something, it is a subterranean fight of words. When there is a clear winner, what we speak is crystal. Else we might end up speaking weird word fusions.
